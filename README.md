# 📰 WireNews

WireNews is a modern, easy-to-use mobile news app that keeps you updated with breaking news and top headlines from around the world.

---

## ✨ What Can You Do With WireNews?

- 📰 **Read Breaking News**: Get real-time headlines updated continuously throughout the day.
- 🏷️ **Explore Categories**: Easily browse news by topic — Business, Sports, Health, Entertainment, Science, and Technology.
- 🔍 **Search Any Topic**: Search for any keyword or topic to find articles instantly.
- 🔐 **Easy Account Sign-In**: Create an account or sign in with 1-click using your **Google Account** or Email.
- 📌 **Save Favorite Articles**: Bookmark news stories to read them later in your **Saved Articles** list.
- 📤 **Share News**: Share interesting articles directly with your friends and family with a single tap.

---

## 📱 App Features & Experience

- **Clean & Modern Design**: Built for a smooth, distraction-free reading experience.
- **Reliable Network Handling**: Built to load quickly even on mobile data.
- **Instant Search**: Type any keyword and press search to instantly discover related news stories.

---

## 🚀 How to Launch the App

1. Open the project folder in **VS Code** or **Android Studio**.
2. Make sure a virtual device (Simulator/Emulator) is running.
3. Click **Run** (or type `flutter run` in the terminal) to start WireNews!
